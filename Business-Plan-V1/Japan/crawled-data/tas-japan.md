## Welcome to JAPAN
Discover a Japan where tradition and innovation are in perfect harmony – where ancient temples and cutting-edge technology combine and serene landscapes complement vibrant city life. Experience a world and discover the wonders of Japan.
Japan’s four seasons offer a variety of unique experiences. Spring brings cherry blossoms, summer brings lively festivals and nature, autumn brings vibrant foliage and delicious seasonal food, and winter brings snow, hot springs, and winter sports. No matter what time of year you visit Japan, you will experience culture, nature, food, and great excitement.
## SPRING
The season of cherry blossoms:  
Spring in Japan is the season of cherry blossoms. In addition to cherry blossoms, many other flowers, such as plums, peaches, and tulips, are in full bloom, creating a colorful landscape.
[ Read more ](https://tas-japan.net/service/travel-business/group-tours/spring/)
## SUMMER
Summer tourism in Japan is full of captivating festivals and beautiful natural landscapes. Traditional events such as fireworks displays and Bon Odori dances, held in various regions, offer unique experiences that can only be enjoyed in Japan during the summer. In addition, outdoor activities like swimming, hiking, and nature walks are very popular.Japan’s summer is the perfect season to enjoy both its rich culture and stunning nature.

## AUTUMN
Autumn is the season of autumn foliage throughout Japan, and beautiful autumn foliage is recommended to be enjoyed together with hot springs. In Japan, autumn is a particularly good time to enjoy Japanese food such as fresh new rice, fruits, vegetables, mushrooms, and fish.
[ Read more ](https://tas-japan.net/service/travel-business/group-tours/autumn/)
## WINTER
Snowy landscapes and ski resorts  
In the North Area, snowy landscapes and winter sports can be enjoyed. Many ski resorts are world-renowned and popular among foreign tourists. Onsen (hot springs) are perfect for cold winters. The open-air baths with a view of the snowy landscape are exceptional. In addition, in winter, illumination events are held in many parts of Japan, creating beautiful lightscapes at various sightseeing spots.
