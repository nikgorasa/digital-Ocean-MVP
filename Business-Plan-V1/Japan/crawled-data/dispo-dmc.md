## Where We Operate
We cover Japan end-to-end with local teams and vetted suppliers in the main hubs and beyond. Click a city to jump to details or ask for a multicity itinerary.
### Tokyo
Japan’s business capital and a top choice for incentives, conferences, and product launches. Two major airports (HND/NRT) and world-class venues.
  * **Best for:** MICE up to 3,000+, tech/product unveils, luxury incentives
  * **Typical group size:** 30–600 (scales higher with multi-venue splits)
  * **Seasonal notes:** Cherry blossom (late Mar–early Apr) and autumn foliage (Nov) peak—book early
  * **Logistics:** Shinkansen hub; large coach availability; citywide venue network


### Kyoto
Historic capital ideal for cultural programs, heritage venues, and premium VIP hosting.
  * **Best for:** Cultural incentives, leadership retreats, heritage gala dinners
  * **Typical group size:** 20–300 (venue-dependent; strict noise/time rules in heritage zones)
  * **Seasonal notes:** Peak tourism in sakura and koyo; plan permits/after-hours access early
  * **Logistics:** Shinkansen to Kyoto; KIX/ITM airports via Osaka


### Osaka
Commercial hub with excellent value for large meetings and fun incentive dining scenes.
  * **Best for:** Conferences/exhibitions, foodie incentives, cost-efficient large groups
  * **Typical group size:** 40–1,000+
  * **Seasonal notes:** Spring/autumn busy; strong hotel inventory year-round
  * **Logistics:** KIX international gateway; easy Kyoto/Nara add-ons


### Nagoya
Manufacturing powerhouse suited to technical visits, dealer meetings, and factory tours.
  * **Best for:** Industrial/automotive programs, dealer incentives, study tours
  * **Typical group size:** 20–350
  * **Seasonal notes:** Flexible availability outside national holidays
  * **Logistics:** NGO airport; Shinkansen node between Tokyo/Osaka


### Hokkaido
Nature-first programs with alpine resorts and winter experiences around Sapporo/Niseko.
  * **Best for:** Winter incentives, outdoor/team-building, wellness retreats
  * **Typical group size:** 15–250 (resort-dependent)
  * **Seasonal notes:** Ski high season Dec–Mar; summer festivals Jun–Aug
  * **Logistics:** CTS airport; private transfers; coach capacity varies by resort


### Fukuoka
Gateway to Kyushu with modern venues and famous open-air food culture (yatai).
  * **Best for:** Regional conferences, foodie incentives, multicity West Japan tours
  * **Typical group size:** 20–400
  * **Seasonal notes:** Pleasant spring/autumn; summer festivals draw crowds
  * **Logistics:** FUK airport minutes from city; Shinkansen to Hiroshima/Osaka


### Okinawa
Tropical resort chain perfect for reward trips, beach galas, and incentive relaxation.
  * **Best for:** Incentives, reward programs, wellness & beach events
  * **Typical group size:** 15–300 (island/resort capacity dependent)
  * **Seasonal notes:** Typhoon season watch (Aug–Sep); shoulder seasons offer value
  * **Logistics:** OKA airport; inter-island hops; resort coach/minivan fleets
