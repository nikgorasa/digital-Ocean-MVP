# About Us
## Your Gateway to Authentic Japan.
## Who we are
A Touch of Japan is an accredited Japan-based DMC founded in 2013. Being located outside a big city ourselves, we aim to bring visitors outside of major tourist hubs and promote the revitalization of rural areas and introduce our guests to parts of Japan that are still undiscovered by mass tourism.  
  
We specialize in crafting luxury tours for FITs and all sized groups, from off-the-beaten-path rural discoveries to Japan-wide package tours. We work with local guides and partners to ensure that our tours benefit the people and small businesses in each area. 
![](https://i0.wp.com/atouchofjapan.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/PHOTO-2025-06-30-13-24-32.jpg?fit=1537%2C2048&ssl=1)
