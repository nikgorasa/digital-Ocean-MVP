## About Japan
![](https://www.ilovejapandmc.jp/assets/images/services-3.jpg)
The Four Seasons
Japan has four distinct seasons, represented by cherry blossoms, fresh verdure, red and gold autumn foliage, and snow. Based on these natural phenomena that distinctly represent the four seasons of spring, summer, autumn, and winter, the people of Japan have cultivated a lifestyle rich in cultural diversity. Flower viewing, firework displays, snow festivals, illuminations and other events to mark the various seasons exist across the country, delighting not just the local residents but also a large number of tourists. The same locations have dramatically different atmospheres in different seasons. 
![](https://www.ilovejapandmc.jp/assets/images/services-1.jpg)
Urban Landscapes
In Japan high-rise districts with towers and buildings built using the latest in construction technology soaring skyward exist alongside historical buildings and lush natural spaces to make for beautiful urban landscapes. This is one of the attractions Japan has to offer as a tourist destination. There are also fashion districts and areas famous for otaku and other kinds of pop culture, as well as old neighborhoods preserved as cultural assets – urban spaces and settings themselves have become tourist attractions in Japan. 
![](https://www.ilovejapandmc.jp/assets/images/services-2.jpg)
Technology
Japan is famous for technology – cars and bikes made by companies like Suzuki, Honda and Toyota; Shinkansen（bullet train） that incorporate a range of leadingedge technologies. Japanese-made products are world-renowned for their high quality and reliability –“ MADE IN JAPAN” is a trademark of safety and dependability. In recent years, technology museums, which offer a glimpse into facilities and manufacturing processes, have increased in number and become popular participatory tourist attractions for both children and adults. 
![](https://www.ilovejapandmc.jp/assets/images/services-4.jpg)
Nature
Japan, an island country surrounded by ocean on all sides, also has a lot of mountainous terrain, and is blessed by an abundance of natural beauty – large rivers cutting across plains, valleys surrounded by mountains on all sides, lush highlands covered with forests. Cherry blossoms and azaleas are viewed in the spring; therapeutic walks in forests verdant with new leaves are a summertime activity; autumn is a time to view beautiful fall colors; and in the winter, a variety of winter sports such as skiing can be enjoyed. The fresh, locally grown seasonal vegetables and fruits are delicious.
