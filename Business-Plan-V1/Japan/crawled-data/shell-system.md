## Service Flow
Five steps from initial inquiry to arrangement completion  
with reliable support

### Initial Inquiry
Confirmation of travel purpose, budget, number of participants, and detailed requests
02
### Quotation & Availability Check
Checking availability of accommodations, transportation, tourist facilities and preparing quotation
03
### Procurement & Itinerary Creation
Creating detailed itinerary, coordinating with service providers, preparing formal quotation
04
### Reservation Confirmation & Contract
Contract signing, confirming all reservations, preparing necessary documentation
05
### Arrangement Completion
Final confirmation, voucher issuance, handover to travel agency
