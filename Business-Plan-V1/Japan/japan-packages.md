## IMPORTANT NOTES

1. **Visa:** Indians need visa for Japan — apply 2-3 weeks in advance
2. **JR Pass:** Consider including 7-day JR Pass ($200) for multi-city trips
3. **Cash:** Japan still cash-heavy — help customers withdraw at 7-Eleven ATMs
4. **Language:** English limited outside tourist areas — guide essential
5. **Cherry Book:** Book 3-4 months in advance for Mar-Apr travel
