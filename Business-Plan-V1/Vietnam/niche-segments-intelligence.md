### Safety Tips (Key)
1. Use Grab for all transport
2. Keep bag on opposite side of traffic
3. Break large bills at chain stores
4. Don't accept "free" items from strangers
5. Use VPN on public WiFi
6. Carry fake wallet for emergencies
7. Book tours through trusted websites
8. Download offline Google Maps
