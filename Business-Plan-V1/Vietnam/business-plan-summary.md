## Executive Summary

GoRASA launches direct-partnership travel packages to Vietnam, targeting Indian travelers with **Ex-Flights + With Flights options**. All pricing in USD for stability. We serve 5 segments: Solo, Women, Elderly, Family, and Group. eSIM included in all packages ($5 avg cost).
