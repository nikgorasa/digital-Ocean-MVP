## Pre-Meeting Checklist

- [ ] Review their TripAdvisor/Google reviews
- [ ] Note 2-3 specific tours they offer
- [ ] Prepare 3 package options (Budget/Mid/Premium in USD)
- [ ] Have commission structure ready (15-25%)
- [ ] Know your customer profile (Indian, $500-2,500 budget)
- [ ] Prepare questions list
- [ ] Test WhatsApp connectivity
- [ ] Have translation app ready (if needed)
- [ ] Review their India-specific offerings
- [ ] Check their vegetarian/Jain meal capability
- [ ] Know eSIM partnership status ($5 avg cost)

## Key Questions for DMCs

### Partnership (USD Pricing)
1. What's your commission structure in USD?
2. What's your minimum group size for discounts?
3. Can you provide white-label packages?
4. Do you have an agent portal/B2B system?

### Operations
5. What's your cancellation policy?
6. What's your response time for bookings?
7. Do you have WhatsApp support for travelers?
8. Can you customize packages for Indian preferences?

### Value-Add
9. Do you offer visa assistance ($25-50)?
10. Do you have eSIM/connectivity packages ($5-10)?
11. Can you provide 24/7 emergency support?
12. Do you have insurance partnerships?

### Niche Segments
13. Do you have women-only tour options?
14. Can you accommodate Jain/Vegetarian meals guaranteed?
15. Do you have family packages with kid-friendly activities?
16. Can you provide scuba diving add-ons ($35-80)?

## Negotiation Tips (USD Context)

### When DMC Asks for Higher Commission
- "We can discuss performance-based bonuses after 10 bookings"
- "Volume guarantee: 20+ bookings/month = +2% commission"
- "Exclusive partnership for Da Nang = higher commission"

### When DMC Asks for Exclusivity
- "Non-exclusive for first 3 months, then we can discuss"
- "Exclusive for Da Nang only, not all Vietnam"
- "Performance clause: $X bookings/month for exclusivity"

### When DMC Asks for Advance Payment
- "We prefer Net 30 after booking confirmation"
- "First 5 bookings: 50% advance, then Net 30"
- "Credit limit: $X per month, reviewed quarterly"
