### Booking Tips
- Book 2-4 weeks in advance for best prices
- Use Trip.com (English-friendly) or Ctrip
- HSR preferred for Beijing-Shanghai and Beijing-Xi'an (cheaper, city center to center)
- Flight recommended for Beijing-Chengdu (too long by train)
