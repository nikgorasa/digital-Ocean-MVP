## INTRODUCTION SCRIPT (15-20 min call)

### Opening (2 min)
```
"Hello [Name], thank you for taking the time to speak with me today.

I'm [Name] from [Company], an Indian travel platform specializing in curated packages for Indian travelers.

We currently operate in Vietnam, Thailand, and Bali, and we're now expanding to China.

I'd love to learn more about your services and explore how we might work together."
```

### Company Overview (3 min)
```
"Let me share a quick overview of what we do:

- We create 10 package types targeting different Indian segments
- Solo travelers, women-only groups, families, and senior citizens
- All packages include eSIM, vegetarian meal options
- We handle end-to-end booking — hotel, flights, activities, visa
- Our average booking value is $800+
- We're looking for 500 bookings in Year 1 for China"

```

### Questions to Ask (10 min)
```
1. "What China destinations do you specialize in?"
2. "Do you have experience handling Indian group tours?"
3. "Can you accommodate vegetarian/Jain meal requirements?"
4. "What's your minimum group size for discounts?"
5. "Do you offer white-label or B2B pricing?"
6. "What's your cancellation and payment policy?"
7. "Do you have DMC licenses for all China regions?"
8. "Can you provide references from Indian travel agents?"
9. "What makes your service different from competitors?"
10. "How quickly can you provide a custom quote?"
```

### Closing (2 min)
```
"This has been very helpful, [Name].

Based on our conversation, I think there's strong potential for partnership.

I'd like to send you:
- Our package portfolio
- Expected monthly booking volume
- Our partnership terms

Could we schedule a follow-up call next week to discuss specifics?

Thank you again for your time."
```

## OBJECTION HANDLING

### "We don't work with Indian agents"
```
"I understand. Many Chinese DMCs started with European markets.

We're different because:
- We guarantee minimum 10 pax per booking
- We handle all visa processing
- We provide confirmed payments upfront
- We can start with a trial period of 3 months

Would a trial partnership work for you?"
```

### "We need higher margins"
```
"Our model is volume-based. We offer:
- Guaranteed 500 bookings in Year 1
- Consistent monthly orders
- Quick payment terms (7 days)
- Marketing support on our platform

Let's discuss a volume-based pricing structure."
```

### "Vegetarian meals are difficult"
```
"Indian travelers are particular about food. We understand.

Our requirements:
- Separate vegetarian menu (not just removing meat)
- Jain options (no onion, garlic)
- Halal certification if possible

We can provide sample menus from our Vietnam partners for reference."
```

### "Visa processing is complex"
```
"True, India-China visa requires documentation.

We handle:
- All paperwork on our end
- Group visa applications (faster processing)
- Pre-approved traveler lists

We just need the DMC's invitation letter."
```
