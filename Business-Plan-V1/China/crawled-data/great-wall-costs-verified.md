## Section-Specific Costs (CNY → USD @ 7.2)

### Badaling Great Wall
- Entry: CNY 40 (Apr-Oct) / CNY 35 (Nov-Mar) = ~$5-6
- Cable car round-trip: CNY 140 = ~$19
- Transport: CNY 30 (subway + bus 877)
- Total budget: CNY 200-350 ($28-49)

### Mutianyu Great Wall
- Entry: CNY 40 = ~$6
- Cable car round-trip: CNY 140 = ~$19
- Cable car + toboggan: CNY 200 = ~$28
- Transport: CNY 86 (subway + tourist bus)
- Total budget: CNY 260-460 ($36-64)

### Simatai Great Wall
- Entry: CNY 40 = ~$6
- Combo with Gubei Water Town: CNY 170 = ~$24
- Transport: CNY 102 (subway + tourist bus)
- Total budget: CNY 350-700 ($49-97)

### Jinshanling Great Wall
- Entry: CNY 65 (Apr-Oct) / CNY 55 (Nov-Mar) = ~$8-9
- Cable car available
- Transport: CNY 106 (subway + tourist bus)
- Total budget: CNY 350-450 ($49-63)

### Juyongguan Great Wall
- Entry: CNY 40 (Apr-Oct) / CNY 35 (Nov-Mar) = ~$5-6
- Transport: CNY 40 (subway + bus 919)
- Total budget: CNY 200 ($28) — closest to Beijing

## Key Notes
- Book tickets online in advance (especially Forbidden City)
- Forbidden City requires mandatory guide
- Bring CNY 200+ for water/snacks
- Cable car costs add $19-28 per person
- Best seasons: Apr-May, Sep-Oct
- Avoid Oct 1-7 (Golden Week — extreme crowds)
