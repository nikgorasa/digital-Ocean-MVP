**Source:** Trip.com, ExamineChina, ChinaAirlineTravel, Reddit
**Date Crawled:** June 21, 2026

## Key Findings

| Route | Typical Fare (CNY) | USD Range | Flight Time |
|-------|-------------------|-----------|-------------|
| Beijing → Shanghai | 500-1,200 | $70-170 | 2.5 hrs |

| Shanghai → Xi'an | 450-1,000 | $63-140 | 2.5 hrs |

### Important Notes from Reddit
- Domestic flights in China can be surprisingly expensive
- Booking online in advance can save 20-60%
- High-speed rail often cheaper and more convenient for routes under 4 hours
- Chinese domestic airlines: Air China, China Eastern, China Southern, Sichuan Airlines, Hainan Airlines

### Booking Platforms
- Trip.com (English-friendly)
- Ctrip (Chinese version of Trip.com)
- Fliggy (Alibaba's travel platform)
- Skyscanner for price comparison

- Beijing-Shanghai: Recommend HSR (better value, city center to city center)
- Beijing-Chengdu: Flight recommended (long distance)
- Shanghai-Chengdu: Flight recommended
