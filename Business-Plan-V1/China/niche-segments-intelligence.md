| Budget | $700-1,400 |
| Duration | 6-10 days |

5. Finding reliable Indian restaurants

| Indian restaurant stops | Comfort food backup |
| Vegetarian cooking class | Cultural immersion |

| City | Restaurant | Type |
|------|-----------|------|

| Shanghai | SongYue Lou | Vegetarian |
| Xi'an | Muslim Quarter | Halal/veg options |
| All | Buddhist Temple Restaurants | Most reliable |

### Essential Phrases Card (Mandarin)
- "I am vegetarian" — Wǒ shì sùshí zhě (我是素食者)
- "No meat, no fish" — Bù yào ròu, bù yào yú (不要肉，不要鱼)
- "Is there chicken in this?" — Zhè dào cài yǒu jī ròu ma? (这道菜有鸡肉吗?)

### Marketing Messages
- "Vegetarian in China? We've got you covered"
- "Buddhist temples serve the best veg food in China"
- "Your vegetarian China survival kit"
- "Eat well, travel far"

### Pricing Premium
- +10-15% for vegetarian-specific curation
- Higher margin due to specialized knowledge
